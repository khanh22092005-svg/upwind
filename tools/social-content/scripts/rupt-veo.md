# RUPT: one video (Gemini + Veo 3 + your voice + CapCut)

**Goal:** a 9:16 video of about 55 seconds. The viewer learns that **rupt = gãy, vỡ** and remembers 5 words:
bankrupt, erupt, interrupt, disrupt and corrupt. The ending is a quiz the viewer can solve alone (abrupt), which
gets comments.

**How it's made:** Veo makes the pictures (8 silent shots, no text). You record the voice. CapCut adds the text,
subtitles and music.

Etymology (checked):

| word | parts | literal meaning |
|---|---|---|
| bankrupt | Italian *banca rotta* | broken bench (the moneylender's counter) |
| rupt | Latin *rumpere, ruptus* | break, burst |
| erupt | e/ex (out) + rupt | burst out |
| interrupt | inter (between) + rupt | break in between |
| disrupt | dis (apart) + rupt | break apart |
| corrupt | cor/com (completely) + rupt | broken completely, rotten → morally spoiled |
| abrupt | ab (off) + rupt | broken off → sudden |

"The bench was smashed when the moneylender went broke" is a story people tell. The voiceover says *tương truyền*
(legend says) so we don't present it as fact.

## The shots

Times are approximate. Cut each Veo clip (8 s) to the length of your voice line.
In the voiceover, *italic* words are English and are read as English.

### Shot 1 · Hook (0:00–0:05)

**Veo:**
> Close-up of an old wooden bench in a tiny Renaissance Italian market. Gold coins are stacked on it. Suddenly the
> bench cracks in the middle and snaps in two in slow motion, and the coins spill and bounce across the stone floor.
> Camera slowly pushes in. Sound: loud wooden crack, coins jingling. [style line]

**On screen:** BANKRUPT = cái bàn gãy?

**Voice:**
> *Bankrupt* là phá sản.
> Nhưng nghĩa đen của nó là… chiếc bàn bị gãy!

### Shot 2 · The story (0:05–0:12)

**Veo:**
> A tiny clay Italian moneylender's counter-bench in a busy miniature market square, coins and a ledger on top.
> An angry hammer comes down from above and smashes the bench; splinters fly. Camera static, wide shot. Sound:
> crowd murmur, one heavy smash. [style line]

**On screen:** banca rotta · bàn · gãy

**Voice:**
> Ở Ý ngày xưa, người đổi tiền ngồi bên một chiếc bàn, gọi là *banca*.
> Tương truyền, ai vỡ nợ thì bàn bị đập gãy: *banca rotta*.

### Shot 3 · The root (0:12–0:18)

**Veo:**
> Extreme close-up of a dry clay twig held between two small clay hands, bending slowly until it snaps in the
> middle, with tiny crumbs flying. Ultra slow motion. Sound: sharp snap. [style line]

**On screen:** **RUPT** = gãy, vỡ

**Voice:**
> Phần *rupt* nghĩa là gãy, vỡ.
> Nắm gốc này, bạn đoán được nghĩa của cả một họ từ.

### Shot 4 · erupt (0:18–0:26)

**Veo:**
> A small clay volcano on a cream island. It rumbles, then bursts upward and outward with bright orange clay lava
> and puffs of smoke. Camera tilts up with the burst. Sound: deep rumble, then a boom. [style line]

**On screen:** **e** (ra ngoài) + **rupt** → **erupt** = phun trào, bùng nổ
*Violence erupted after the election.*

**Voice:**
> *E* nghĩa là ra ngoài.
> Vỡ bung ra ngoài: *erupt*, phun trào, bùng nổ.
> *Violence erupted after the election.*

### Shot 5 · interrupt (0:26–0:34)

**Veo:**
> Two clay dominoes on a cream table, a smooth blue ribbon of light flowing from one to the other like a
> conversation. A third orange clay block drops down between them and cuts the ribbon in the middle; the ribbon
> breaks into two loose ends. Side view, static camera. Sound: soft hum, then a sharp clack. [style line]

**On screen:** **inter** (ở giữa) + **rupt** → **interrupt** = ngắt lời, cắt ngang
*Please don't interrupt me while I'm speaking.*

**Voice:**
> *Inter* là ở giữa.
> Chen vào giữa làm gãy mạch: *interrupt*, ngắt lời.
> *Please don't interrupt me while I'm speaking.*

### Shot 6 · disrupt (0:34–0:42)

**Veo:**
> A neat clay toy train running on a circular track through a tiny clay town. A crack splits the track and the
> carriages tip and scatter in different directions, gently, like toys. Top-down camera slowly rotating. Sound:
> train clatter, a crack, toys tumbling. [style line]

**On screen:** **dis** (tách ra) + **rupt** → **disrupt** = làm gián đoạn, đảo lộn
*The strike disrupted train services nationwide.*

**Voice:**
> *Dis* là tách ra.
> Vỡ tung mỗi mảnh một nơi: *disrupt*, làm gián đoạn, đảo lộn.
> *The strike disrupted train services nationwide.*

### Shot 7 · corrupt (0:42–0:50)

**Veo:**
> A shiny orange clay apple on a cream pedestal, perfect on the outside. Cracks spread across its skin and it
> slowly crumbles from the inside into dark dust, keeping its shape until the last moment. Slow push-in. Sound:
> soft crackling, a quiet crumble. [style line]

**On screen:** **cor** (hoàn toàn) + **rupt** → **corrupt** = thối nát, tham nhũng
*Corrupt officials should be punished severely.*

**Voice:**
> *Cor* là hoàn toàn.
> Vỡ nát từ bên trong: *corrupt*, thối nát, tham nhũng.
> *Corrupt officials should be punished severely.*

### Shot 8 · Quiz and loop (0:50–0:58)

**Veo:**
> A clay rope bridge stretches across a small gap between two cream cliffs. Without warning the rope snaps clean off
> at one end and the bridge drops away. Camera holds still on the empty gap for two seconds. Sound: rope creaking,
> a sudden snap, then silence. [style line]

**On screen:** **ab** (rời ra) + **rupt** → **abrupt** = ?
A. đột ngột · B. chậm rãi · C. rõ ràng
"Bình luận đáp án!"

**Voice:**
> Thử nhé: *ab* là rời ra.
> Vậy *abrupt* là gì?
> Bình luận đáp án của bạn nhé!

**Pinned comment:** Đáp án A: *abrupt* = đột ngột (gãy rời ra bất ngờ). *The meeting came to an abrupt end.*

**Loop:** end on the empty gap with the snap sound. It rhymes with the bench snapping in shot 1, so the video
feels like it starts again.

## Recording the voice

- Use a phone in a small room with soft things around (a closet or a bedroom), 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- Read each line separately, leaving 1 second of silence between lines, so it's easy to cut.
- Say the English words in clear, natural English, a little slower than the Vietnamese.
- Use an energetic tone in shot 1 and a slower, curious one in shot 8.

## Editing in CapCut

1. Put the voice down first. Cut out the silences, but keep about 0.3 s after each comma and 0.6 s after each full stop.
2. Lay the Veo shots over the voice and trim each one to its line. Keep Veo's sound effects at about 30% volume.
3. **Text:** the word parts appear one by one, then snap together into the word. Colours: prefix orange #f2711c,
   rupt deep blue #004b9b, the whole word in white on a blue pill. Example sentences go smaller, in the lower third.
4. **Subtitles:** auto-captions, then fix every English word by hand. Show one line at a time.
5. **Music:** quiet and royalty-free, very low, dipping under the voice.
6. **Last frame:** "UpWind · Học từ từ gốc" plus "Bình luận GỐC TỪ để nhận ebook".

## Checks before posting

- Watch it muted. Does the on-screen text alone teach the 5 words?
- Do the first 3 seconds make you want to keep watching?
- Does no Veo shot show letters or garbled text? If one does, regenerate it.
- Ask 2–3 students to watch it, then an hour later ask which words they remember.
