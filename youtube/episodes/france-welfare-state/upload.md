## Description
France's streets are full again. More than half a million people marched on 17 October 2026 as school protests merged with anger over a €54 billion austerity budget. Behind it is a bigger question: can France still afford the most generous welfare state in Europe, and is the model itself breaking?

We look at the numbers: public spending, pensions, debt, demography, and how Denmark and Sweden run big welfare states without big deficits.
