# Will France's crisis put the welfare-state model in question?

Research notes for the sample episode (checked 2026-10-21). Every number in script.json comes from this list.

## Now: the October 2026 protests
- 17 Oct 2026: ~550,000 marched nationwide (Interior Ministry); PM Lecornu: 1,200 arrested, 150 police injured; fire largely destroyed Limoges' historic town hall. Began last month in high schools in Paris' northern suburbs over teacher shortages and poor facilities; spread to Bordeaux, Rennes, Nantes, Lyon, Lille, Marseille; coincided with a public-sector strike against the budget. — [Al Jazeera, 18 Oct 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/why-have-frances-school-protests-become-a-wider-political-crisis)
- 2027 budget: €54bn of savings and tax measures; freeze of the civil-service pay scale (unions: 4th year in a row); freeze of all but the lowest pensions; smaller tax deduction for retirees; without it the 2027 deficit would exceed 6.5% of GDP; target 5%; an extra €10bn needed because of rising rates and geopolitical tension. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/why-have-frances-school-protests-become-a-wider-political-crisis), [France 24, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.france24.com/en/france/20261001-french-pm-to-present-belt-tightening-2027-budget-including-frozen-wages-and-new-taxes), [Euronext/Reuters](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/france-plans-eu54-billion-savings-drive-protests-grow)
- Lecornu: "We can no longer finance everything." — [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/europe/we-can-no-longer-finance-everything-french-premier-says-as-government-seeks-spending-cuts/4058337)

## The model
- Public spending 57.2% of GDP in 2025 (INSEE); about 7.4 points above the euro-area average. — [INSEE](https://www.insee.fr/en/statistiques/8961174), [Kyiv Post analysis](https://www.kyivpost.com/analysis/87630)
- Public social spending just over 30% of GDP in 2024, highest in Austria, Finland and France. — [OECD social spending](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/social-spending.html)
- Public pensions ~13–14% of GDP, among the highest in the OECD. — [OECD Pensions at a Glance 2025](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pensions-at-a-glance-2025_e40274c1-en/full-report/public-expenditure-on-pensions_ddc9a2dd.html)
- Pensions replace ~72% of pre-retirement earnings vs ~55% Germany, ~50% US (OECD net replacement rates, cited by Kyiv Post). France's spending gap with the euro area comes mostly from pensions (+2.2 pts) and health (+1.5 pts). — [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/analysis/87630), [Crédit Agricole](https://etudes-economiques.credit-agricole.com/en/french-deficit-and-politics-too-late-get-out-woods)
- No budget surplus since 1974. — [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/analysis/87630)

## Politics
- 2023 reform raised the retirement age 62 → 64; suspended in the 2026 social-security budget until after the 2027 presidential election, the price of Socialist support. Cost €0.4bn in 2026, €1.8bn in 2027. — [France 24, 16 Dec 2025](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20251216-french-lawmakers-adopt-social-security-budget-suspend-macron-s-flagship-pension-reform)
- Hung parliament since the 2024 snap election; Barnier government fell Dec 2024, Bayrou government Sep 2025; presidential election April 2027. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/why-have-frances-school-protests-become-a-wider-political-crisis)

## Disputed / to say carefully
- Whether the problem is the size of the welfare state or how it is financed: Nordic countries spend heavily too but run small deficits (Sweden's automatic pension balancing, Denmark's retirement age tied to life expectancy).
