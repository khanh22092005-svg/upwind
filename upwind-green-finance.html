The Problem

You're not behind because you're not good enough.
You're behind because nobody told you this earlier.

The hiring system is broken for students. It rewards experience you can only get by already having experience. Here's what that actually looks like — and why it's not your fault.

→ 01

Nobody hires students without experience. But how do you get experience without being hired?

It's the loop that wastes years. You apply, get filtered out, and don't know why — until you realise the only missing thing was evidence. Not talent. Evidence.

→ 02

What you learn in uni and what the job actually needs are two different things.

You can ace every exam and still freeze on day one. Real work requires judgment under pressure — not just knowing the right answer, but finding it when no one hands you the question.

→ 03

By the time most students wake up to this, they're already two years behind.

While you were told to "focus on studies", others were quietly collecting projects, internships, and LinkedIn connections. That head start is very real — and very hard to catch.

→ 04

Most internships don't actually teach you anything you'd want to show a hirer.

Making coffee. Sitting in meetings. "Helping out." We've all heard the stories. Your time is worth more than an internship you can't talk about in an interview.
