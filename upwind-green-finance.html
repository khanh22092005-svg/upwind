01 / The Gap

Why do students with great grades still get filtered out at the résumé stage?

→ 01

The experience catch-22

Employers want candidates with experience. You need a job to get experience. That loop traps most students for years.

→ 02

Classroom vs. reality

University gives you theory. The real job demands analysis, judgment, and delivering under pressure — a gap best closed early.

→ 03

Starting in year 3–4

By the time most students start looking, others already have 2–3 real projects on their CV. That head start is hard to catch.

→ 04

Misaligned internships
