The Problem

You're not losing to better candidates.
You're losing to candidates with more evidence.

Grades matter. Clubs look fine. But when a recruiter has 200 CVs, they're scanning for proof you can do real work — and most students just don't have it.

→ 01

The loop: you need experience to get experience.

Companies want candidates who've done the work. But you can only do the work if someone hires you. Most students spend 2–3 years stuck in this before they figure a way out.

→ 02

University teaches you to pass exams. Jobs need something else.

Writing a real recommendation, defending your analysis under pressure, producing work with a deadline — none of that gets taught in lectures. The gap between campus and company is real.

→ 03

If you're waiting until year 3 to start, you're already behind.

While you're waiting, students who started in year 1 are quietly building 2–3 projects in their CV. By the time you're applying for the same roles, that head start is nearly impossible to close.

→ 04

Most internships don't give you anything to show for it.

Making coffee. Shadow-following. Doing tasks you can't describe in an interview. Your time is worth more — and Upwind is built specifically around work you can actually talk about.
