## Reading | Matching headings | Cam 20 Test 3 Passage 2 | https://engnovate.com/ielts-reading-tests/cambridge-ielts-20-academic-reading-test-3/ | options i-vii
> Câu 14–19: chọn heading i–vii cho từng đoạn A–F. Đọc tóm tắt 1 dòng mỗi đoạn trước, rồi mới chọn.
14 Section A = v | Đoạn A nêu hai mục tiêu của sở thú: “SHOW PEOPLE how wonderful they are” và “HIGHLIGHT the research and conservation efforts” → v (Two clear educational goals). | T1
15 Section B = ii | Đoạn B: “This comfortable SYMBIOTIC RELATIONSHIP has led to the growth of coral reefs” → ii (Cooperation beneath the waves). | T1
16 Section C = iv | Đoạn C: so sánh “RAINFORESTS OF THE SEA” bị “DISMISSED BY SOME naturalists” → iv (Disagreement about the accuracy of a certain phrase). | T1
17 Section D = vii | Đoạn D: “scientists ADVISE that MORE THAN 90% of reefs COULD BE LOST BY 2050” → vii (A warning of further trouble ahead). | T1
18 Section E = iii | Đoạn E: “scientists ARE TRYING to pinpoint hardy types of coral that could survive” → iii (Working to lessen the problems). Heading i (Tried and tested solutions) không dùng: các cách này còn đang thử, chưa “tried and tested”. | T5
19 Section F = vi | Đoạn F: cho công chúng thấy tiến bộ để “ENCOURAGE THEM TO BELIEVE that we can do something” → vi (Promoting hope). | T1
