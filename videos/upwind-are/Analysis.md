# Reference analysis
Source: https://www.tiktok.com/@englishprotips/video/7682803194816236822 (references/source.mp4, 67s, 1080x1920, 23.976fps)
Used only to understand format. No footage, script, or likeness of the creator is reused.

## Why it works (structure)
1. Cold open skit (0-12s): one presenter plays two roles. "Examiner" in blazer + glasses reads a Part 1 question
   (boxed dark caption, "IELTS Examiner" tag). "Candidate" in a casual tee gives a one-line answer, then an awkward
   silent stare. The silence is the joke and the problem.
2. Teach (12-40s): to-camera in a third outfit. Key words pop in bold coloured captions (red/pink/blue).
   An acronym builds as a persistent stack in the top-left: letters first, then each word fills in
   on the word that explains it.
3. Redo (40-67s): same question, candidate now answers fully. The completed acronym stays on screen and
   each line highlights as the answer hits that step (Respond -> Elaborate -> Example). Captions return to
   plain white boxes, so the stack is the thing your eye follows.

## Visual systems
- Role caption: dark box with white bold text and word-by-word reveal (examiner); white box (candidate).
- Teach caption: big outlined sans, keyword in colour, no box.
- Acronym stack: top-left, persistent from first mention until the end, active line coloured.
